1. Introduction
1.1 Purpose of the System
The purpose of this system is to digitize and improve the management of IT hardware assets and their maintenance activities, which are currently handled through manual, paper-based, and Microsoft Excel records. It provides a centralized platform for registering, tracking, assigning, dispatching, receiving, maintaining, and monitoring IT hardware assets across branches and IT support locations.
1.2 Background
Currently, hardware inventory information at CBE branches is maintained using Microsoft Excel and physical files. This makes it difficult to:
Obtain centralized and real-time information.
Search records as asset volume increases.
Track equipment dispatch, receipts, and maintenance history.
Determine current asset locations or responsible personnel quickly.
1.3 Objectives
The main objectives of the CBE IT Hardware Inventory Management System include:
Digitizing IT hardware records and reducing Excel/paper dependence.
Tracking the complete lifecycle of hardware assets.
Improving accountability for hardware movement and assignments.
Managing and maintaining hardware maintenance history.
Generating inventory and maintenance reports with search and filter capabilities.

2. System Scope
2.1 What the System Does
The system manages the lifecycle of IT hardware from registration and deployment through assignment, dispatch, maintenance, reassignment, retirement, or disposal. It supports asset identification, technical inspection, hardware problem reporting, and audit logging.
2.2 What Problems it Solves
By transitioning to a centralized web-based database, the system solves the problem of decentralized records, missing maintenance histories, unverified equipment receipts, and slow manual report generation. It ensures every IT hardware asset is explicitly linked to its location, current status, and responsible person.

3. Overall System Description
3.1 System Overview
The proposed system is a centralized web-based application built on a three-tier architecture (Next.js frontend, NestJS API, PostgreSQL database). It allows users to track the entire asset workflow: from initial receipt and technical inspection, to branch assignment, maintenance loops, and eventual retirement.

The system implements Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) to restrict features based on the user's responsibilities. The primary roles are the System Administrator, IT Inventory Officer, Hardware Technician, and Branch Manager.

6. Functional Requirements Summary
The functional requirements define the core behaviors of the system.
6.1 User & Branch Management
The system shall securely authenticate users and provide role-based access. Administrators can create, update, and deactivate users, as well as manage branch location profiles.
6.2 Asset Management
Authorized users can register hardware with unique CBE Asset Tag Numbers to prevent duplicates. The system records asset attributes (e.g., serial number, model, condition) and maintains real-time status tracking (Available, Assigned, In Transit, Under Maintenance, etc.).
6.3 Assignment Management
The system manages the assignment of equipment to employees or branches. It logs the assignment date, the responsible individual, and retains previous assignment history.
6.4 Dispatch & Receipt Process
Users can dispatch equipment to a destination branch. The system tracks receiver details and captures receipt confirmations directly within the dispatch workflow, changing the asset status accordingly.

6.5 Maintenance Management
The system handles the entire maintenance loop:
Problem Reporting: Branch Managers log hardware issues directly tied to a specific asset.
Technical Inspection: Technicians record diagnosis, required parts, software/OS installations, and security checks.
History: Every maintenance action is permanently appended to the asset's historical record.
6.6 Reporting & Dashboards
Users receive customized dashboards displaying relevant metrics (e.g., pending requests, assets in transit). The system generates exportable PDF/Excel reports covering inventory counts, maintenance histories, and dispatch logs.
6.7 Other Functions
Search and Filtering: Advanced search by tag, serial, branch, or status.
Attachment Management: Uploading supporting documents (e.g., repair proofs, receiver signatures).
Audit Logging: Tracing critical data modifications to the specific user and IP address.

8. Business Rules and Constraints
8.1 Business Rules
BR-01: Every asset shall have a strictly unique CBE Asset Tag Number.
BR-03: An asset cannot possess conflicting active statuses.
BR-04: Equipment cannot be actively assigned to two different employees simultaneously.
BR-05: An asset must be officially registered in the system before it can be dispatched.
BR-06: Maintenance requests must be linked directly to an existing registered asset.
BR-07: A dispatched asset requires destination confirmation before it becomes fully active at that location.
BR-08: Equipment exiting maintenance must have a technical evaluation and condition logged before reassignment.
8.2 Constraints & Assumptions
Assumption: Authorized users possess valid employee IDs, and branches will supply initial data for migration.
Constraint: The system will not integrate with core CBE financial or enterprise banking systems initially.
Constraint: Access to production data and real hardware during the initial development/internship phase is limited.

10. Conclusion
The CBE IT Hardware Inventory Management System is designed to replace fragmented, manual Excel and paper-based inventory workflows with a secure, centralized digital platform. By focusing strictly on IT hardware lifecycles, it ensures accurate asset registration, transparent dispatch tracking, and comprehensive maintenance logging.
The resulting system will significantly improve asset accountability, reduce lost maintenance histories, streamline reporting processes, and provide management with real-time visibility into the organization's technological infrastructure.
