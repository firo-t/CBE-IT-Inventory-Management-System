1. INTRODUCTION
1.1 Purpose
The purpose of this Software Requirements Specification (SRS) is to define the functional and non-functional requirements of the proposed CBE IT Hardware Inventory Management System.
The system is intended to digitize and improve the management of IT hardware assets and their maintenance activities currently handled through Microsoft Excel, paper-based records, and manual processes.
The system will provide a centralized platform for registering, tracking, assigning, dispatching, receiving, maintaining, and monitoring IT hardware assets across branches and IT support locations.
It will also maintain the history of hardware-related maintenance activities and provide reports to authorized users.
1.2 Project Scope
The CBE IT Hardware Inventory Management System will manage the lifecycle of IT hardware from registration and deployment through assignment, dispatch, maintenance, reassignment, retirement, or disposal. Equipment may be received by the IT support team from a branch for maintenance or technical preparation.
The system will support:
Hardware registration
Asset identification and tagging
Hardware technical inspection
Inventory management
Asset assignment
Equipment dispatch
Equipment receipt confirmation
Maintenance completion and dispatch back to the responsible branch
Hardware problem reporting
Maintenance management
Asset dispatch and assignment tracking
Asset status tracking
Search and filtering
Reports and statistics
User and role management
Audit logging
Attachment/document management
The system will focus specifically on IT hardware asset tracking, assignment, dispatch, and hardware-related maintenance. It is not a warehouse stock, purchasing, or procurement management system.
It will not attempt to replace the complete CBE Incident Management or IT Service Management platform.

The current process makes it difficult to obtain centralized and real-time information about hardware assets and their history.
2.2 Problems with the Existing System
The following problems were identified:
Hardware records are mainly maintained using Excel.
Records can become difficult to search as the number of assets increases.
Asset dispatch and movement are manually tracked.
Equipment dispatch and receipt confirmation records are not centralized.
Maintenance history is difficult to follow.
It is difficult to determine the current location of an asset quickly.
It is difficult to identify who is currently responsible for an asset.
Report generation requires manual processing.
Paper-based receiver information and signatures can be difficult to manage.
There is limited centralized historical tracking of asset activities.
Maintenance and asset history are difficult to track manually.
Hardware problems reported by branches/customers need to be connected with the corresponding hardware asset.
3. OBJECTIVES
The main objectives of the system are:
To digitize IT hardware inventory records.
To reduce dependence on Excel and paper-based records.
To provide centralized hardware information.
To track the complete lifecycle of hardware assets.
To improve accountability for hardware movement.
To track asset assignment and location.
To manage hardware maintenance activities.
To maintain maintenance history for each asset.
To simplify equipment dispatch and receipt confirmation.
To provide search and filtering capabilities.
To generate inventory and maintenance reports.
To provide role-based access.
To maintain an audit trail of important system activities.

10. BUSINESS RULES
BR-01 Unique Asset Identification
Every asset shall have a unique CBE Asset Tag Number.
BR-02 Unique Serial Number
The system should prevent duplicate serial numbers where the organization's asset policy requires uniqueness.
BR-03 Asset Status
An asset cannot have conflicting active statuses.
For example, an asset marked "Under Maintenance" should not simultaneously be marked "Available".
BR-04 Assignment
An asset should not be actively assigned to two different employees at the same time.
BR-05 Dispatch
An asset must exist in the system before it can be dispatched.
BR-06 Maintenance
Maintenance requests must be linked to an existing asset, and each maintenance request shall have one associated maintenance record.
BR-07 Dispatch Receipt Confirmation
A dispatched asset shall be confirmed by the destination receiver before it becomes fully active at the destination.
BR-08 Maintenance Completion
Equipment completing maintenance shall have its condition and maintenance result recorded before it is dispatched back or made available for reassignment.
BR-09 Authorization
Only authorized users can perform sensitive operations.
BR-10 Auditability
Important asset changes must be recorded in the audit log.

30. CONCLUSION
The CBE IT Hardware Inventory Management System will provide a centralized solution for managing the lifecycle of IT hardware assets.
The system will replace fragmented manual inventory processes with a structured digital platform that supports asset registration, assignment, dispatch and receipt confirmation, maintenance, hardware problem reporting, asset movement, reporting, and auditing.
The system is intentionally focused on IT hardware inventory and hardware-related maintenance rather than attempting to replace the organization's complete IT Incident Management or IT Service Management platform.
The proposed architecture using a web-based frontend, RESTful backend, and relational database will provide a maintainable and scalable foundation for future expansion.
